  Use only when the user asks what their next meeting/appointment is, what's coming up next, or when their next meeting with a specific person is — with no specific day mentioned. Needs no date arguments — it finds the soonest upcoming synced event from the real current time, narrowed to the given person when "persons" is set (as in the second example above, for "when's my next meeting with alex?"). Do NOT use calendar_list_appointments or calendar_search_appointments for a "next meeting (with X)" question and try to compute date_from/date_to yourself — you cannot reliably compute "now" as a Unix timestamp, and calendar_list_appointments has no person filter at all, so it would just return whatever appointment happens to be listed first, unrelated to the person asked about. Do NOT use this for "first meeting today/tomorrow" or any question naming a specific day — use list_today_appointments or list_tomorrow_appointments for those instead, since "first" there means the earliest event on that day, not the overall next event.
